design rationale for main layout

split the viewport into a sticky profile panel and a scrollable content panel. this context anchoring basically ensures the user doesnt forget who they are reading about when they are 5 miles deep into chronological data

it also completely avoids cognitive overload. instead of giving people a complex nav header with 6 different routes (which sucks for quick scanning) we just dump them into a curated linear flow
